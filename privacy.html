Privacy

Your keys.
Your control.

Your actions are stored on your iPhone. The keys you choose go directly to the Mac you pair over Bluetooth.

Effective October 5, 2026

Data not collected by the app.

Pocket Keys for Mac stores your three key assignments, custom action names, symbols, key combinations, and sound/haptic preferences in local app storage on your iPhone.

The app has no account, app server, advertising, or analytics SDK. It does not upload your actions or usage to the publisher, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Bluetooth input.

With your permission, the app advertises as a Bluetooth keyboard and sends the key presses and key releases you configure to a Mac you pair through the operating systems. Bluetooth reports intentionally leave the iPhone and are received by your paired Mac.

The operating systems manage discovery, pairing records, and Bluetooth security. Nearby devices may see the advertised app name or your iPhone’s Bluetooth name.

Pocket Keys for Mac does not read the Mac’s screen, documents, clipboard, or physical keyboard. It does not receive confirmation that the Mac’s focused app completed a command.

Sound and haptics.

Key sounds are bundled with the app. Playing them does not record microphone audio. Sound and haptics happen locally and can be disabled in Edit → More → Key feel. Sound follows the iPhone’s silent mode.

The Test key in that section auditions feedback on your iPhone and sends no keyboard input to your Mac.

Your choices.

You can withdraw Bluetooth permission in iPhone Settings. The app keeps the screen awake while it is in the foreground and restores normal Auto-Lock when you leave. This does not provide background keyboard operation. Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation. Bluetooth pairing records are separate: use Forget This Device on the iPhone and remove the iPhone from the Mac’s Bluetooth Settings to remove the pairing.

Your operating system’s backup and restore settings may retain or restore local app data. The publisher does not operate those backups. App Store purchases, operating-system services, and system diagnostics are managed by Apple under Apple’s own policies.

This website and support.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its static pages have no forms, JavaScript, advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or remote images. These pages do not set cookies.

GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving GitHub Pages. GitHub may process other service and usage information under its Privacy Statement. See GitHub Pages data collection for its hosting disclosure. GitHub’s services and cookies are separate from the Pocket Keys app.

Public support requests.

Support uses the public Pocket Keys support repository on GitHub. If you submit an issue or comment, your GitHub profile, message, and attachments are visible to the developer and the public. Posting requires a GitHub account. The developer uses information you voluntarily share to respond to support requests and understand reported problems.

Public discussions can remain available after a request is answered. You can edit your contributions using GitHub’s available controls. To request removal of an issue or other support content, contact the repository owner through the support channel and identify the content without reposting sensitive details. GitHub controls its own records and retention under its Privacy Statement.

Do not include passwords, pairing codes, private documents, or other sensitive information in a public request. Website access logs and voluntary public support submissions are separate from the app’s local storage and Bluetooth input. The app’s Data Not Collected statement does not mean GitHub’s website and support services process no data.
